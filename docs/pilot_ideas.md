# Pilot ideas

## Status of this file

This is an idea notebook, not established canon. Names, appearances,
relationships, personalities, and ship assignments may change. Moving an idea
into production should be a deliberate game-design decision, not something an
agent infers from its presence here.

All pilots must remain original to Codename Hover. Working names also need an
ordinary name and trademark clearance pass before public marketing or release.

## Regular Jaguar

Regular Jaguar is the baseline character from which Cyber Jaguar contrasts. The
word "regular" is deliberately part of the working concept: this version should
feel more grounded, approachable, and less technologically extravagant.

Currently established:

- working name: Regular Jaguar;
- role: pilot;
- relationship: the less flashy counterpart to Cyber Jaguar;
- final appearance, personality, history, voice, and ship are undecided.

## Cyber Jaguar

Cyber Jaguar is the cooler, more technologically extreme version of Regular
Jaguar. "Cooler" should eventually become visible through a strong silhouette,
presentation, attitude, and vehicle pairing rather than merely higher numerical
stats.

Currently established:

- working name: Cyber Jaguar;
- role: pilot;
- relationship: an enhanced or more spectacular counterpart to Regular Jaguar;
- final appearance, personality, history, voice, and ship are undecided.

## Relationship questions

Do not choose among these possibilities without owner direction:

- the same person before and after cybernetic enhancement;
- alternate versions of one person;
- two rivals using related identities;
- an original and an imitation;
- an entirely different relationship.

## Template for future pilot ideas

When an idea becomes detailed enough, record:

- working and display name;
- one-sentence hook;
- silhouette, clothing, helmet, colors, and readable visual motifs;
- personality and motivation;
- relationship to other pilots and the game world;
- associated ship, without duplicating the ship's gameplay definition;
- animation, portrait, voice, and audio needs;
- unresolved choices and explicit owner feedback.

Dynamic in-race portrait behavior is specified separately in
`pilot_portraits.md`; keep character identity here and reusable presentation
mechanics there.
